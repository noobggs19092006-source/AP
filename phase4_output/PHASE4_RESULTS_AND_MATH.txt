3. SETUP AND DEFINITIONS  [DERIVED]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Raw dimension d = 24 x 12 x 11 = 3168 per sample.  Training mean tensor M[a,b,c] = (1/N_train) sum_i X[i,a,b,c]  (float64; stored).  Z_i = X_i - M for every split.
Matrix PCA/SVD: A = flatten_C(Z_train) in R^{24108 x 3168} (C order: index = a*132 + b*11 + c).  G = A^T A (float64, streamed in batches).  Eigen-decompose G = V diag(lambda) V^T,
  lambda_1 >= ... ; singular values sigma_j ~ sqrt(max(lambda_j,0)).  Feature f_PCA = vec(Z_i) V_k;  reconstruction X_hat = M + reshape(f V_k^T).
Shared HoSVD (sample mode uncompressed): mode Grams G_time (24x24), G_station (12x12), G_feature (11x11) accumulated over all training samples; leading eigenvectors give Q_time, Q_station, Q_feature.
  Core C_i = Z_i x_time Q_time^T x_station Q_station^T x_feature Q_feature^T  (shape r_t x r_s x r_f);  F_i = vec_C(C_i) with index p*(r_s*r_f) + q*r_f + r.
  Inverse Zhat_i = C_i x_time Q_time x_station Q_station x_feature Q_feature;  X_hat_i = M + Zhat_i.  Einsum index order: 'iabc,ap->ipbc', 'ipbc,bq->ipqc', 'ipqc,cr->ipqr'.
  Equivalent flat form: vec(C_i) = vec(Z_i) K with K = Q_time (x) Q_station (x) Q_feature (Kronecker, d x k, orthonormal columns); this was used as the independent direct path in tests/audit subsets.
Sign convention: each factor column is flipped so its largest-|.| entry is non-negative, BEFORE any feature export.
Metrics: SSE = ||Z-Zhat||_F^2, RRE = sqrt(SSE/||Z||_F^2), MSE = SSE/(N*3168), retained_energy = ||Zhat||_F^2/||Z||_F^2, all accumulated in float64 per split.
  PRIMARY metrics use explicit reconstruction from FULL-PRECISION (float64) internal codes.  The *_float32_codes columns repeat the metric decoding the float32-EXPORTED codes.
  The float32 and float64 RREs differ by at most 2.08e-15 (absolute)  [MEASURED], i.e. immaterial for every reported digit.

4. THEOREMS USED  [DERIVED]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
T1 (projection identity).  P = K K^T with K orthonormal (PCA: K=V_k; HoSVD: K=Kronecker factor).  Then Z - PZ is orthogonal to PZ, so SSE = ||Z||^2 - ||PZ||^2 = ||Z||^2 - ||codes||^2, hence RRE^2 + retained_energy = 1.
   Float32 storage of F breaks this only at ~1e-7 relative level in the stored codes; the code verifies the identity in float64 against explicit reconstruction.
T2 (mode eigenvalues).  The mode-t Gram G_t = Z_(t) Z_(t)^T where Z_(t) is the mode-t unfolding of the whole training collection (sample mode appended to the columns).  Its eigenvalues are the squared
   singular values of Z_(t); trace(G_t) = ||Z_train||_F^2 for all three modes and for the flat Gram (all verified numerically).
T3 (HoSVD training truncation bound).  Let P_t, P_s, P_f be the orthogonal projectors Q Q^T acting on the time, station and feature modes.  They act on different tensor modes, so they commute, are
   self-adjoint and idempotent in the Frobenius inner product, and Zhat = P_t P_s P_f Z.  Telescoping,
       Z - Zhat = (I-P_t)Z  +  P_t (I-P_s) Z  +  P_t P_s (I-P_f) Z.
   The three terms are mutually orthogonal (e.g. <(I-P_t)Z, P_t W> = <Z,(I-P_t)P_t W> = 0, and similarly using (I-P_s)P_s = 0 and commutation), hence
       ||Z-Zhat||^2 = ||(I-P_t)Z||^2 + ||P_t (I-P_s)Z||^2 + ||P_t P_s (I-P_f)Z||^2  <=  ||(I-P_t)Z||^2 + ||(I-P_s)Z||^2 + ||(I-P_f)Z||^2,
   because projectors are contractions.  Finally ||(I-P_t)Z||^2 = ||Z_(t)||^2 - ||Q_t^T Z_(t)||^2 = sum_{j>r_t} lambda_j(G_t) = tail_time (Q_t spans the top-r_t eigenspace of G_t); same for the other modes.
       ||Z_train - Zhat_train||_F^2  <=  tail_time(r_t) + tail_station(r_s) + tail_feature(r_f).   (classical truncated-HOSVD argument; sample mode uncompressed)
   The first term equals tail_time EXACTLY; the other two are bounded by their tails.  The bound depends on TRAIN spectra and is NOT claimed for validation/test.
T4 (PCA optimality on TRAIN).  By Eckart-Young-Mirsky / Ky Fan, among all rank-k orthogonal projectors P on R^d, ||A - A P||_F^2 is minimized by the span of the top-k right singular vectors,
   with minimum sum_{j>k} lambda_j.  HoSVD reconstructs flat(Z) K K^T, a projection onto a particular k-dimensional (Kronecker-structured) subspace; therefore
   SSE_PCA,train(k) <= SSE_HoSVD,train(k) for exact PCA at equal k.  This is a statement about TRAIN reconstruction of the flattened training matrix only; it implies nothing about validation/test reconstruction or classification.
T5 (numerics of the Gram route).  Eigenvalues of the float64 Gram are exact for the COMPUTED Gram, not an infinite-precision SVD of A; forming A^T A squares the condition number, so eigenvalues
   below roughly n*eps*lambda_max are not resolved.  Declared policy: tol = n*eps64*lambda_max; eigenvalues < -tol raise an error; eigenvalues in [-tol,0) are clipped to 0 and counted/logged.

11. INTERPRETATION AND CAVEATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Overlapping windows.  Consecutive 24-hour windows share 23 of 24 hours, so samples are strongly dependent: the effective sample size is much smaller than N, errors/metrics are weighted by repeated hours, and
    no standard errors or confidence intervals are reported (they would need a block bootstrap, not done here).  Differences of a few thousandths in RRE should not be over-interpreted.
(b) Units.  Reconstruction errors are in Phase-2 standardized units (per station-feature train z-scores); they are not ug/m^3 and not directly comparable across channels' physical importance.  Channels with
    duplicated meteorology (Section 2) carry multiplicity weight in every Frobenius quantity.
(c) Scope of optimality.  Exact PCA's optimality (T4) holds only for TRAIN reconstruction of the flattened centered training matrix.  On held-out splits PCA had the lower RRE in 6 of 6 (validation/test x config) comparisons [MEASURED]; that is an empirical observation, and the held-out PCA-minus-HoSVD gap is not guaranteed by T4.  The HoSVD bound (T3) uses TRAIN spectra and is not a held-out guarantee.
(d) Train-fitted 4-way HoSVD with an uncompressed sample mode is equivalent to a Tucker-type model with identity sample factor; shared factors are fixed after training, so it is a frozen linear map for new windows.
(e) HoSVD is a NON-optimal truncation (T3 only bounds it): successive/simultaneous mode projection is not the best multilinear rank-(r_t,r_s,r_f) approximation; no HOOI/ALS refinement was run (out of scope).
(f) Lower reconstruction error is NOT evidence of better next-state classification.  Phase 5 owns accuracy, macro-F1, state-change recall, entry-into-high-state recall, calibrated probabilities, and paired comparison
    against the Phase 3 Markov persistence baseline (~90.75% Scope-A test accuracy, 0 recall on true regime changes -- a figure quoted from the brief, not recomputed here).
(g) Not done: any supervised model, kernel/RFF, Markov fusion, threshold tuning, or selection on test; no literature novelty claim is made.
