# Graph and diagram audit

20 of 20 numerical graph-source checks pass. Each category has a CSV and PNG/PDF output. Metrics, reliability bins, feature summaries and cross-phase prediction comparisons are independently recalculated. Historical compression and validation-grid charts are checked against their preserved source tables; old decompositions are not retrained. Bar artist heights/widths are checked against plotted numbers.

Six diagrams were rendered from vector primitives. The Markov matrix values are preserved in a separate CSV. Architecture and decision diagrams reflect actual branching, including missing-state fallback and E6 label decisions. Representative scientific plots and the Markov diagram were visually inspected; no claim of a separate pixel-by-pixel audit of every image is made.
